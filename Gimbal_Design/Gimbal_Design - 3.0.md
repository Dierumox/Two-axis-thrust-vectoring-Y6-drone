# Gimbal design - 3.0
The 2.0 version didn't solve how to power the electronics held on the gimbal rings, and most importantly, the main thrust motors. The 3.0 version introduces the data transmission slip rings for the first time, along with the brushless gimbal motors, the magnetic encoder to provide position feedback for each gimbal ring, and the CNC conductive bearings to carry the main power line.
